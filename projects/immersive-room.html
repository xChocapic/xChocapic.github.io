360° immersive room + ESP32 scent diffusers

Nov 2025 – Jan 2026 · Howest team project for occupational therapy · team of 3 · product owner

You describe a place out loud. About a minute later you are standing in it: an 8K 360° panorama on four projectors, with matching sound and scent.

One-minute cut. Full 4-minute demo (14 MB).

How it works

  1. Azure Speech transcribes what you say.
  2. GPT-4o-mini rewrites it into an image prompt.
  3. ComfyUI on a campus GPU generates a seamless 360° panorama (SDXL + a 360 LoRA), upscaled to 8192×4096.
  4. Unity shows it on four projectors with ambient sound.
  5. Four ESP32 units switch scent diffusers: beach, forest, garden, mountain.

My part

Product owner, the whole backend, the electronics and the documentation. I designed and programmed the four scent diffusers (ESP-IDF C, MQTT over TLS) and 3D-printed their housings. The Unity front end was built by my two teammates.

Stack

.NET 8 Azure Functions, Azure OpenAI, Azure Speech, Blob Storage, IoT Hub, ComfyUI, ESP-IDF, Unity 6.
