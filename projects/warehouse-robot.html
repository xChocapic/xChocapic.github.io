Warehouse forklift robot simulation

April 2026 · 3D Programming final project · solo

A simulated forklift robot that finds its way through a warehouse, recognises the pallet it needs from a camera image, and picks it up.

[video: screen capture]

How it works

  • Grid A* pathfinding with path smoothing and replanning.
  • A control state machine with motor torque, braking and fork control.
  • Vision loop: the robot camera image goes to a backend for YOLO detection, then the robot backs off, scans, verifies and re-inserts the forks.

Model

YOLO fine-tuned on a synthetic dataset generated in Unity. Six symbol classes, mAP50 0.823 and mAP50-95 0.775 on 364 validation images. Exported to ONNX.

Stack

Unity 6 (about 6,100 lines of C#), FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, Docker, Ultralytics YOLO, ONNX.
