Connected touch lamps

May – June 2026 · personal project, built with my partner

Two lamps for two people. Touch one, and the other starts breathing slowly for five minutes.

[video: one lamp triggering the other]

How it works

  • Two ESP32s paired over Bluetooth Classic: one is the server, the other connects to it by address.
  • The breathing follows a resting breathing rate: a 4 s cosine fade that never goes fully dark.
  • Holding a sensor for 10 s shows solid red for a minute as a low-battery signal.
  • The client lamp retries the connection every 5 s, and both lamps keep working on their own while disconnected.

Hardware

Per lamp: one ESP32, four capacitive touch sensors, two 12-LED NeoPixel rings, a 3D-printed body.
