Drone-based airfield lighting inspection

May – June 2026 · S4 industry project for Intersoft Electronics · team of 4

A night drone flight over a runway approach becomes a health map of every ground light: which lamps work, which are off, and which could not be judged.

[video: drone demo]

How it works

  1. YOLO + SAHI detects lights in each of the 715 frames.
  2. Camera rays are projected back onto the ground using the drone's PPK positioning.
  3. A small residual MLP corrects the projected positions.
  4. Detections are matched to the surveyed lamps within 12 m.
  5. Each lamp is classified by colour and health.

Result

529 lamps working, 5 off or missing, 4 not assessable. 96–97% identification accuracy.
